## Regulatory Chain

The Korean maintenance-organisation framework should be analysed through:

1. Aviation Safety Act
2. Enforcement Decree of the Aviation Safety Act
3. Enforcement Rule of the Aviation Safety Act
4. MOLIT standards, notices and administrative rules
5. AMO approval requirements and inspection guidance
6. AMO Certificate
7. Operations Specifications
8. Approved organisation manuals and procedures
9. Maintenance records and objective evidence
10. MOLIT/KOCA surveillance and findings

## AMO Approval Domains

The applicable Korean requirements should be mapped against:

- Application and issue of AMO approval
- Ratings and approved scope
- Aircraft and component limitations
- Approved maintenance locations
- Facilities and work environment
- Personnel qualification and authorisation
- Tools, equipment and calibration
- Maintenance data
- Components, materials and stores
- Performance and certification of maintenance
- Organisation manuals and procedures
- Quality or compliance-monitoring system
- Safety Management System
- Record retention and reporting
- Changes to the organisation
- Findings, enforcement and continued validity

## Approval and Scope Evidence

Control the following records separately from the legislation:

| Record | Purpose |
|---|---|
| AMO Certificate | Evidence that an approval was issued |
| Operations Specifications | Approved ratings, scope, limitations and locations |
| Approved organisation manual | Approved management and maintenance procedures |
| Capability list | Detailed maintenance capability, where applicable |
| Personnel authorisations | Individual certification privileges |
| Approval correspondence | Conditions, changes, limitations and authority decisions |

A valid certificate does not independently confirm every rating, aircraft limitation, location or maintenance capability. Operations Specifications and associated approved documents must also be checked.

## Audit Evidence

Typical evidence includes:

- Current AMO Certificate
- Current Operations Specifications
- Approved scope and locations
- Approved organisation manual and amendments
- Personnel licences, qualifications and authorisations
- Competence and training records
- Facilities and environmental controls
- Tools, equipment and calibration records
- Current maintenance data
- Stores and component records
- Work packages and maintenance-release records
- Occurrence and safety reports
- Internal audits and quality-monitoring records
- Findings, RCA, CAPA and effectiveness evidence
- MOLIT/KOCA correspondence and surveillance records

## Language and Translation Control

Where Korean and English versions are available:

- Preserve the Korean official title.
- Record the English translated title separately.
- Identify whether the English text is official or informational.
- Use the Korean official legal text as the controlling source when required.
- Record translation uncertainties as `VERIFICATION REQUIRED`.

## Source-Control Note

Do not mark Article 97 or related implementing provisions as `VERIFIED — CURRENT` until the current official Korean text, effective date, delegated provisions and English translation status have been checked.

Applicability and compliance must be assessed separately.
