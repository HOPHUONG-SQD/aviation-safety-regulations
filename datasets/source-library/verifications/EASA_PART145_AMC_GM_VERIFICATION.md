## 6. Regulatory Status

ED Decision 2026/005/R states that:

- Acceptable Means of Compliance are non-binding standards used to demonstrate compliance
- Guidance Material is non-binding material supporting interpretation
- Amendment 9 supports amendments introduced through Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/100
- The Decision applies from 07 August 2026

AMC and GM must therefore be distinguished from the mandatory requirements contained in Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014 and Annex II, Part-145.

An organisation may use an applicable AMC to demonstrate compliance or may propose an Alternative Means of Compliance where permitted and accepted through the applicable process.

## 7. Detailed Amendment 9 Review

### 7.1 Amendment presentation convention

The official Amendment 9 document states that:

- Deleted text is struck through
- New text is highlighted
- An ellipsis indicates that unchanged text has been omitted

The amendment document contains two Part-145 provision-level changes.

### 7.2 Provision-level changes

| Change ID | Provision | Verified amendment | Primary applicability | Matrix interface |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AM9-P145-01 | AMC2 145.A.60 — Occurrence reporting | AMC2 145.A.60 is deleted in full | Part-145 organisations and occurrence-reporting guidance | AMO-14 |
| AM9-P145-02 | AMC1 145.B.300(f)(c)(iv) — Oversight principles | Authority-oversight wording is revised from physical surveys or partial airworthiness reviews to investigations performed in accordance with point M.B.901 | Competent authority oversight | AMO-21 / authority oversight interface |

### 7.3 AMC2 145.A.60 deletion

Amendment 9 deletes the complete text of AMC2 145.A.60.

The deleted AMC previously addressed sharing relevant safety-related occurrence reports with the design approval holder or the declarant of a declaration of design compliance and liaison concerning service information or solutions.

The deletion must not be interpreted as:

- Deleting point 145.A.60
- Removing mandatory occurrence-reporting obligations
- Removing internal safety-reporting obligations
- Permitting an organisation to discontinue occurrence analysis or follow-up
- Establishing VAECO compliance or non-compliance

The mandatory requirements must continue to be assessed from the current consolidated Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014, applicable occurrence-reporting legislation and the organisation’s approved procedures.

### 7.4 AMC1 145.B.300(f)(c)(iv) amendment

The amended provision concerns the competent authority’s oversight principles.

The revised wording refers to investigations performed by the competent authority in accordance with point M.B.901 where the results indicate an issue originating from a Part-145 organisation.

This change is primarily authority-facing. It is relevant to VAECO only through the oversight interface and does not create a standalone organisation requirement merely because the AMC wording changed.

## 9. VAECO Follow-up Boundary

The Amendment 9 review supports the following document checks:

- Verify that the controlled MOE does not present deleted AMC2 145.A.60 as current AMC text
- Verify that occurrence-reporting procedures remain traceable to current mandatory requirements
- Retain the distinction between external occurrence reporting and internal safety reporting
- Record AMC1 145.B.300(f) as an authority-oversight interface
- Do not create a finding solely because Amendment 9 changed or deleted AMC wording

Organisation compliance has not been assessed in this verification pass.
