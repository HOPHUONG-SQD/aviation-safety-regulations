## 3. Primary Applicability

### 14 CFR 145.1

Part 145 establishes:

- The process for obtaining a repair station certificate
- Rules applicable to certificated repair stations
- Requirements concerning maintenance, preventive maintenance and alterations
- Applicability to persons holding or required to hold a Part 145 certificate

## 5. Key Verified Requirements

### 145.5 — Certificate and Operations Specifications

A repair station may not operate without, or contrary to:

- Its Repair Station Certificate
- Its ratings
- Its Operations Specifications

The certificate and Operations Specifications must be available at the premises for inspection.

### 145.51 — Application

An application includes, as applicable:

- Repair Station Manual
- Quality Control Manual
- Article list
- Organisation chart and management information
- Housing and facility description
- Contract-maintenance functions
- Training programme

Required equipment, personnel, technical data, housing and facilities must be available for FAA inspection.

### 145.53 — Issue of Certificate

A qualifying applicant may receive a certificate with:

- Appropriate ratings
- Operations Specifications
- Safety-related limitations

Additional conditions apply to repair stations located outside the United States.

### 145.55 — Duration and Renewal

For a repair station outside the United States:

- The certificate normally expires on the last day of the 12th month after issue.
- The FAA may renew it for 24 months when the applicable conditions are met.
- The renewal request must normally be submitted no later than 30 days before expiration.

### 145.59 and 145.61 — Ratings

Part 145 establishes:

- Airframe ratings
- Powerplant ratings
- Propeller ratings
- Radio ratings
- Instrument ratings
- Accessory ratings
- Limited ratings
- Limited ratings for specialised services

A limited rating may cover nondestructive inspection, testing and processing.

### 145.103 — Housing and Facilities

Controls include:

- Sufficient workspace
- Segregation and protection of articles
- Separation of hazardous or sensitive operations
- Suitable storage and protection
- Separation of stock from work in progress
- Suitable ventilation, lighting, temperature and humidity

### 145.109 — Equipment, Materials and Data

The repair station must control:

- Required equipment, tools and materials
- Calibration of test and inspection equipment
- Manufacturer-recommended or FAA-acceptable equivalent equipment
- Current and accessible technical data

Technical data include:

- Airworthiness Directives
- Instructions for Continued Airworthiness
- Maintenance manuals
- Overhaul manuals
- Standard-practice manuals
- Service bulletins
- Other applicable FAA-approved or acceptable data

### 145.151–145.157 — Personnel

The repair station must provide:

- An accountable manager
- Sufficient qualified personnel
- Sufficient supervisors
- Competent inspection personnel
- Qualified personnel authorised to approve articles for return to service

For repair stations outside the United States, Part 145 contains specific experience, training and competence provisions that differ from requirements for domestic repair stations.

### 145.161 — Personnel Records

Required records include:

- Management and supervisory roster
- Inspection-personnel roster
- Return-to-service authorisation roster
- Employment summaries supporting experience requirements

Rosters must reflect specified personnel changes within five business days.

### 145.163 — Training

The repair station must:

- Have and use an FAA-approved employee training programme
- Include initial and recurrent training
- Ensure personnel can perform assigned tasks
- Document individual training
- Retain required training records for at least two years
- Submit programme revisions through the required process

### 145.201 — Privileges and Limitations

The repair station may perform and approve work only:

- Within its ratings
- Within Operations Specifications limitations
- With necessary technical data, equipment and facilities
- In accordance with Part 43

### 145.205(d) — Line Maintenance

FAA-approved line maintenance requires:

- Work performed under the applicable operator manual and approved maintenance programme
- Necessary equipment
- Trained personnel
- Required technical data
- Line-maintenance authorisation in the repair station’s Operations Specifications

### 145.207 and 145.209 — Repair Station Manual

The repair station must:

- Prepare and follow an FAA-acceptable manual
- Keep the manual current
- Make it accessible to applicable personnel
- Provide it to the responsible Flight Standards office
- Control revisions
- Include the required procedures and organisational information

### 145.211 — Quality Control System

The repair station must establish and maintain an FAA-acceptable Quality Control System ensuring the airworthiness of maintained articles.

The Quality Control Manual includes procedures for:

- Incoming-material inspection
- Preliminary inspection
- Hidden-damage inspection
- Inspection-personnel proficiency
- Current technical data
- Qualification and surveillance of noncertificated contractors
- Final inspection and return to service
- Calibration
- Corrective action on deficiencies
- Forms and manual revision

### 145.213 — Inspection and Maintenance Release

Before return to service:

- The maintained article must be inspected.
- The inspector must determine airworthiness regarding the work performed.
- The maintenance release must provide the required certification.
- Inspection personnel must meet Section 145.155.

### 145.215 — Capability List

For a limited-rated repair station:

- The article must be within the certificate rating.
- The article must appear on the current capability list or Operations Specifications.
- A documented self-evaluation must confirm capability before adding an article.

### 145.217 — Contract Maintenance

Contract-maintenance controls include:

- FAA approval of contracted functions
- Maintenance of contractor information
- Equivalent quality controls for noncertificated persons
- Repair-station responsibility and direct control
- Test or inspection before return to service

### 145.219 — Records

The repair station must:

- Retain records in English
- Demonstrate compliance with Part 43
- Provide the maintenance release to the owner or operator
- Retain records for at least two years
- Make records available to the FAA and NTSB

### 145.221 — Service Difficulty Reports

A serious failure, malfunction or defect discovered by the repair station must generally be reported to the FAA within 96 hours, subject to the provision’s conditions and exceptions.
