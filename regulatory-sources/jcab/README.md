## Regulatory Chain

The JCAB maintenance-organisation framework should be analysed through:

1. Civil Aeronautics Act
2. Ordinance for Enforcement of the Civil Aeronautics Act
3. MLIT and JCAB circulars, standards and guidance
4. Approved Organization requirements
5. Organisation approval certificate
6. Approved ratings, scope and limitations
7. Approved organisation manuals
8. Maintenance procedures and records
9. JCAB surveillance, findings and corrective actions

## Approved Organization Framework

The applicable Japanese requirements should be mapped against:

- Application and approval
- Approved organisation categories
- Ratings and scope of work
- Aircraft, engine and component limitations
- Approved maintenance locations
- Facilities and work environment
- Personnel qualification and authorisation
- Inspection and certification personnel
- Tools, equipment and calibration
- Maintenance data
- Components, parts and materials
- Performance and certification of maintenance
- Organisation manuals and procedures
- Quality assurance or compliance monitoring
- Safety Management System
- Maintenance and airworthiness records
- Occurrence and defect reporting
- Changes to the organisation
- Findings, enforcement and continued validity

## Terminology Control

Do not automatically translate the Japanese approval system as `Part-145`.

Use the official JCAB or MLIT terminology found in the applicable legal text, certificate and approval documentation. Record alternative English translations separately where necessary.

## Approval and Scope Evidence

The following organisation-specific records must be controlled separately:

| Record | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Approved Organization Certificate | Evidence that JCAB approval was issued |
| Approval specifications or conditions | Ratings, scope, limitations and locations |
| Approved organisation manual | Management and maintenance procedures |
| Capability list | Detailed maintenance capability, where applicable |
| Personnel authorisations | Individual certification privileges |
| JCAB correspondence | Conditions, changes and authority decisions |

Approval status and approved scope must be verified separately. A valid certificate does not independently establish every maintenance rating, aircraft limitation, location or specialised capability.

## Audit Evidence

Typical evidence includes:

- Current approval certificate
- Approval specifications, scope and limitations
- Approved locations
- Approved organisation manual and amendments
- Personnel licences, qualifications and authorisations
- Competence and recurrent-training records
- Facilities and environmental controls
- Tools, equipment and calibration records
- Current maintenance data
- Components, parts and stores records
- Work packages and maintenance certification
- Occurrence and defect reports
- Internal audits and quality-assurance records
- Findings, RCA, CAPA and effectiveness evidence
- JCAB correspondence and surveillance records

## Language and Translation Control

When using Japanese and English documents:

- Preserve the original Japanese title and reference number.
- Record the English translated title separately.
- Identify whether the English translation is official or unofficial.
- Use the current Japanese legal text as the controlling source where required.
- Record translation uncertainty as `VERIFICATION REQUIRED`.
- Do not establish a finding solely from an uncertain translation.

## Source-Control Note

Do not mark a JCAB source as `VERIFIED — CURRENT` until its current Japanese text, revision, effective date, applicability and translation status have been confirmed.

Applicability and compliance must be assessed separately.
