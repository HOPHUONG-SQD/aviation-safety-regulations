## AMO Mapping Domains

ICAO Annex 8, Part II, Chapter 6 should be mapped against:

- AMO approval and scope
- Maintenance procedures and exposition
- Personnel competence and authorisation
- Human Factors and training
- Facilities and work environment
- Tools, equipment and calibration
- Maintenance data
- Records and release to service
- Quality and compliance monitoring

## Source-Control Note

Do not mark an ICAO source as `VERIFIED — CURRENT` until the current edition, amendment, effective date and applicable provisions have been confirmed from an official ICAO source.
