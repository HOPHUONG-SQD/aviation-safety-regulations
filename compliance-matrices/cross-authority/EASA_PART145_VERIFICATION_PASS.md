## 5. Status and Scope Separation

### Status Dataset

May confirm:

- Approval number
- Organisation name
- Country
- Recorded Valid/Invalid status
- Dataset or retrieval date

It does not independently establish:

- Ratings
- Aircraft limitations
- Line or Base maintenance
- NDT scope
- Approved locations
- Maintenance privileges

### Scope Dataset

May support confirmation of:

- Ratings
- Aircraft types
- Engine or component limitations
- Line and Base maintenance
- NDT or specialised services
- Approved locations

### Certificate and Terms of Approval

These are organisation-specific approval evidence and must be reconciled with the Status and Scope Datasets.

### MOE

The MOE describes the approved organisation, management system, procedures, locations and scope implementation. Its approval and amendment status must be traceable.

## 8. Date Reconciliation Rule

A difference between:

- MOE revision date
- Approval-page signature date
- Certificate revision date
- Status Dataset update date
- Retrieval date

is not automatically a conflict.

The dates must be assessed according to their document-control purpose and traceability.

Assign a conflict only where the records cannot be reconciled or indicate incompatible approval status or scope.
