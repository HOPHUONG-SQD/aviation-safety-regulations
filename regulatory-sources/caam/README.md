## Important Classification Rule

- `CAD 8601` concerns Maintenance Organisation Approval.
- `CAD 6801` concerns Continuing Airworthiness of Aircraft.
- Do not use CAD 6801 as the primary approval standard for a maintenance organisation.
- Continuing-airworthiness requirements may still create interfaces between an operator, CAMO and maintenance organisation.

## Approval and Scope Evidence

The following organisation-specific records must be controlled separately:

- Maintenance Organisation Approval Certificate
- Terms of Approval
- Ratings and limitations
- Approved locations
- Line and base maintenance scope
- NDT or specialised-services scope
- Maintenance Organisation Exposition
- Capability list
- Certifying-staff authorisations

A valid approval certificate does not independently establish every rating, limitation, location or maintenance capability.

## Audit Evidence

Typical evidence includes:

- Current approval certificate and scope
- Approved exposition and revision status
- Personnel qualification and authorisation records
- Competence and recurrent-training records
- Facilities and work-environment controls
- Tools, equipment and calibration records
- Components, materials and stores records
- Current maintenance data
- Maintenance work packages
- Certification and release records
- Occurrence reports
- Internal audits and compliance-monitoring records
- Findings, RCA, CAPA and effectiveness evidence

## Source-Control Note

Do not mark a CAAM source as `VERIFIED — CURRENT` until its exact title, issue, revision, effective date and official document URL have been confirmed.

Applicability and compliance must be assessed separately. Listing a requirement does not demonstrate organisational compliance.
