## VAECO Approval Records

Project-specific records should be maintained separately from the general regulations:

| Record | Control purpose |
|---|---|
| Approval number EASA.145.0775 | Organisation identity and approval-status verification |
| EASA Form 3 Certificate | Approval certificate evidence |
| Terms of Approval | Ratings, limitations and approved scope |
| Status Dataset | Valid or invalid status evidence |
| Scope Dataset | Ratings, aircraft limitations, activities and locations |
| MOE | Approved organisational procedures and scope implementation |
| Approval correspondence | Traceability of authority decisions and changes |

## Status Versus Scope

A Status Dataset may confirm whether an approval is valid or invalid.

It does not necessarily establish:

- Ratings
- Aircraft limitations
- Line or Base Maintenance privileges
- NDT scope
- Approved locations
- Specific organisational privileges

These elements must be confirmed using the approval certificate, Terms of Approval, Scope Dataset, approved MOE and other applicable authority records.

## Audit Evidence

Typical evidence includes:

- EASA Form 3 and Terms of Approval
- Current approved MOE
- Management-system and compliance-monitoring records
- Personnel competence and authorisation records
- Certifying and support-staff records
- Facility and work-environment controls
- Tool and calibration records
- Maintenance data
- Work packages and certificates of release to service
- Occurrence and internal safety reports
- Findings, RCA, CAPA and effectiveness evidence
