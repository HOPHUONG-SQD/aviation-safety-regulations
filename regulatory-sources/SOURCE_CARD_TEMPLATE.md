Select one status:

- `VERIFIED — CURRENT`

## 5. Regulatory Content

### Applicable requirement

Record the exact article, paragraph, section or clause.

### Summary

Provide a concise summary without changing the regulatory meaning.

### Related documents

List implementing regulations, AMC, GM, advisory material, certificates, approval scope or organisational procedures.
