# Regulatory Sources

This directory contains authority-specific regulatory source registers and verified official links for the Aviation Safety Regulations project.

## Authorities

- ICAO
- CAAV — Civil Aviation Authority of Vietnam
- FAA — Federal Aviation Administration
- EASA — European Union Aviation Safety Agency
- CAAM — Civil Aviation Authority of Malaysia
- CAAP — Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines
- MOLIT / KOCA — Republic of Korea
- JCAB — Japan Civil Aviation Bureau

## Required Source-Card Fields

Each regulatory source record should include:

1. Authority
2. Document title
3. Document number
4. Regulatory level
5. Revision or edition
6. Effective date
7. Applicability
8. Official source URL
9. Verification date
10. Verification status
11. Notes

## Controlled Source Status

Use one of the following statuses:

- `VERIFIED — CURRENT`
- `VERIFICATION REQUIRED`
- `SUPERSEDED`
- `REFERENCE ONLY`

## Source Control Rules

- Prioritise official authority sources.
- Verify the document number, revision and effective date.
- Distinguish mandatory regulations from AMC, GM and guidance.
- Do not treat a search result or third-party summary as an official source.
- Record information that cannot be confirmed as `VERIFICATION REQUIRED`.
- Preserve the official URL and verification date.
