## 5. Regulatory Boundary

This register confirms:

- The existence and subject of the identified Part-145 clauses
- The appropriate clause-level mapping for the 26 AMO domains
- The specific management-system references omitted from the initial matrix

This register does not confirm:

- VAECO compliance
- Applicability of every subparagraph to every activity
- Satisfaction of AMC or GM
- Approved ratings, limitations or locations
- Line or Base maintenance privileges
- NDT methods, levels or locations
- Closure of EASA Gate G5

## 6. Status Rules

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| VERIFIED | Clause and regulatory subject identified in Annex II |
| PARTIAL | Regulation identified, but AMC/GM or organisation-specific evidence remains necessary |
| OPEN | Source or requirement has not been sufficiently verified |
| NOT APPLICABLE | Applicability has been assessed and excluded with justification |

A `VERIFIED` clause mapping is not a compliance conclusion.
