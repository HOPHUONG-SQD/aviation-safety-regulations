## 5. Control Rules

- Applicability and compliance must be assessed separately.
- A located provision is not automatically a verified matrix mapping.
- Organisational approval, scope, capability and implementation require separate objective evidence.
- Thông tư 18/2026/TT-BXD must not be used as the sole AMO requirement source.
- No CAAV/VAR matrix row may be marked `VERIFIED` until the relevant provision has been reviewed in context.
- `NO REQUIREMENT → NO FINDING`
