## 4. Verification Limitations

- The applicable edition and amendment of ICAO Annex 8 have not been verified.
- Part II, Chapter 6 has not been reviewed from a controlled source.
- ICAO Annex 1 metadata has been verified, but the applicable aircraft maintenance personnel licensing provisions have not been reviewed from a controlled source.
- ICAO Annex 13 is an accident and incident investigation source and must not be treated as a general AMO compliance requirement.
- Annex 13 investigation findings and safety recommendations must not be treated automatically as AMO audit findings.
- Titles, summaries or secondary references must not be treated as verified provisions.
- No ICAO matrix row may be marked `VERIFIED` or `MAPPED`.
- Existing ICAO references in the Core Matrix remain `verify`.
- ICAO applicability and organisational compliance remain separate assessments.
- `NO REQUIREMENT → NO FINDING`
