## Verification Requirements

Before relying on a regulatory requirement:

1. Open the official authority source.
2. Confirm the exact document title and number.
3. Verify the current revision or edition.
4. Confirm the effective date.
5. Determine applicability to AMO / Part-145, SMS, continuing airworthiness or aircraft maintenance.
6. Record the access and verification date.
7. Mark any unresolved information as `VERIFICATION REQUIRED`.

## Source Priority

- **P0:** Official legislation, regulation, authority decision or approval record
- **P1:** Official AMC, GM, advisory circular, guidance or authority manual
- **P2:** Recognised industry standard or technical publication
- **P3:** Academic research or professional analysis
- **P4:** Unofficial summaries and general reference material
