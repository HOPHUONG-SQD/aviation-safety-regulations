# Regulatory Framework

## Regulatory Chain

The project analyses regulatory information through the following chain:

1. International standards and recommended practices
2. National or regional primary legislation
3. Implementing regulations
4. Authority-specific AMO / Part-145 requirements
5. AMC, GM, advisory material and official guidance
6. Approval certificates, Terms of Approval, scope and OpSpecs
7. Organisation procedures, MOE, SMS manuals and work instructions
8. Objective evidence, audit findings and corrective actions

## Applicability Domains

| Domain | Typical subjects |
|---|---|
| AMO approval | Certificate, scope, ratings, locations and privileges |
| Personnel | Competence, qualification, authorisation and training |
| Facilities | Environment, stores, segregation, access and safety |
| Tools and equipment | Availability, calibration, serviceability and control |
| Maintenance data | Currency, access, amendments and correct use |
| Quality system | Audits, findings, corrective actions and follow-up |
| SMS | Hazard reporting, risk management, assurance and promotion |
| Continuing airworthiness | CAMO, operator and AMO interfaces and records |

## Evidence Rule

A regulatory statement must be traceable to an official source.

A compliance conclusion must:

- Identify the applicable requirement
- Be supported by objective evidence
- Distinguish implementation from effectiveness
- Clearly identify information that has not been verified

## Audit Principles

- No requirement → No finding
- Implementation ≠ Effectiveness
- Effective ≠ Closed
- Conflicting evidence requires verification
