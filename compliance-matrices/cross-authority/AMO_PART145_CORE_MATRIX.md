## 2. Purpose

This matrix maps equivalent or related regulatory subjects across:

- ICAO international standards
- CAAV requirements
- FAA repair-station regulations
- EASA Part-145 requirements

This matrix does not establish organisational compliance and must not be used alone to raise an audit finding.

## 3. Mapping Status

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| MAPPED | Exact applicable provision identified and verified |
| PARTIAL | Related provision identified, but mapping is incomplete |
| VERIFY | Exact provision, currency or applicability requires verification |
| NO DIRECT EQUIVALENT | No direct equivalent identified after review |
| NOT APPLICABLE | Subject is outside the relevant authority framework |

These are source-mapping codes, not compliance codes.

## 5. Evidence Layers

For each mapped requirement, retain evidence across four layers:

| Layer | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Regulatory source | Official law, regulation or binding approval condition |
| Implementation material | AMC, GM, AC, order, user guide or authority instruction |
| Organisation approval | Certificate, OpSpecs, Terms of Approval, scope and approved locations |
| Objective evidence | Procedures, records, interviews, observations and samples |

## 6. Mapping Rules

1. Do not map requirements only because their titles appear similar.
2. Read each provision in its regulatory context.
3. Identify differences in scope, terminology, conditions and exclusions.
4. Separate mandatory requirements from guidance.
5. Record when no direct equivalent exists.
6. Do not convert `VERIFY` into conformity or non-conformity.
7. Verify status and scope separately.
8. Maintain direct official links in the Master Source Register.
9. Record verification activity in the Verification Log.
10. Obtain independent review before using a P0 mapping for a finding.

## 7. Compliance Boundary

This matrix answers:

> Which regulatory provisions address the same or related AMO subject?

It does not answer:

> Is VAECO compliant with those provisions?

Organisational compliance requires a separate applicability assessment and objective evidence.

## 8. Finding Gate

Before using a matrix row to establish a finding:

- Exact applicable requirement must be verified
- Mandatory or approval-binding status must be confirmed
- Exact clause must be recorded
- Organisational applicability must be established
- Objective evidence must demonstrate the gap
