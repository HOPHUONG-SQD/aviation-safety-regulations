## 3. Source Classification

Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014 is mandatory EU implementing legislation.

Annex II to the Regulation contains Part-145 requirements applicable to approved maintenance organisations within its defined scope.

The consolidated EUR-Lex text is used to determine the regulation as amended on the consolidation date.

A consolidated text is a documentation aid. Where formal legal reliance is required, the original act and applicable amending acts published in the Official Journal must also be considered.
