## Regulatory Chain

The CAAP maintenance-organisation framework should be analysed through:

1. Republic Act No. 9497
2. Philippine Civil Aviation Regulations
3. Philippine Civil Aviation Standards
4. Advisory Circulars
5. Memorandum Circulars and Safety Directives
6. AMO Certificate and Operations Specifications
7. CAAP-approved MOE Supplement or organisation manual
8. Organisation procedures and maintenance records
9. Objective evidence and CAAP oversight records

## Approval and Scope Evidence

The following organisation-specific records must be controlled separately:

- AMO Certificate
- Operations Specifications
- Ratings and limitations
- Approved locations
- Line and base maintenance scope
- Specialised-service and NDT scope
- CAAP MOE Supplement
- Capability list
- Certifying-personnel authorisations
- One-off or temporary approval letters

A valid AMO Certificate does not independently confirm every rating, limitation, location or maintenance capability.

## Wet-Lease and Airline-Specific Evidence

Where VAECO supports a Philippine operator, separately verify:

- Contractual maintenance scope
- CAAP acceptance or approval
- Airline MOE or MCM procedures
- Operator-specific training
- Personnel authorisation policy
- Release-to-service limitations
- RII requirements
- Approved stations
- One-off approval or endorsement letters
- Current roster and supporting records

Do not assume that airline authorisation permits release to service when the applicable CAAP or operator policy limits that privilege.

## Audit Evidence

Typical evidence includes:

- Current AMO Certificate and Operations Specifications
- Approved MOE Supplement
- Scope and location approvals
- Personnel licence, competence and authorisation records
- Operator-specific training
- Facilities and station capability
- Tools, equipment and calibration records
- Current maintenance data
- Stores, materials and component records
- Work packages and certification records
- RII records
- Internal audits
- Findings, RCA, CAPA and effectiveness evidence
- CAAP and operator correspondence

## Source-Control Note

Confirm the exact PCAR amendment, effective date and official document before marking any source as `VERIFIED — CURRENT`.

Do not treat an Advisory Circular, Memorandum Circular or airline manual as equivalent to a mandatory PCAR provision unless its legal or approval effect has been established.

Applicability and compliance must be assessed separately.
