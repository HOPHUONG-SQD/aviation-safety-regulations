# Project Charter

## Project Name

Aviation Safety Regulations

## Purpose

Create a controlled repository for researching, comparing and maintaining aviation regulations and guidance relevant to aircraft maintenance organisations, AMO / Part-145 approval, Safety Management Systems, continuing airworthiness and quality auditing.

## Core Authorities

- ICAO
- CAAV
- FAA
- EASA
- CAAM
- CAAP
- MOLIT / KOCA
- JCAB

## Initial Scope

- AMO / Part-145 approval
- Maintenance organisation exposition and quality systems
- SMS requirements applicable to maintenance organisations
- Continuing airworthiness interfaces
- Maintenance personnel authorisation and competence
- Facilities, tools, equipment and calibration
- Audit oversight, findings, RCA, CAPA and effectiveness

## Control Principles

1. Use official and authoritative sources whenever available.
2. Record the exact document title, number, revision and effective date.
3. Preserve the official source URL.
4. Record the verification date and source status.
5. Distinguish mandatory requirements from AMC, GM and guidance.
6. Do not establish a finding without an applicable requirement.
7. Separate implementation evidence from effectiveness evidence.
8. Keep regulatory research separate from organisational compliance conclusions.
